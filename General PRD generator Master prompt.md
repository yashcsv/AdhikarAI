# PHASE 1 — PRODUCT DISCOVERY

Before creating the PRD:

Determine:

## Problem Analysis

* What problem exists?
* Who experiences it?
* How often does it occur?
* How painful is it?
* What are the consequences of not solving it?

## User Analysis

* Who are the primary users?
* Who are secondary users?
* What jobs are they trying to complete?
* What frustrations exist today?

## Existing Alternatives

Identify:

* Existing software
* Existing workflows
* Manual processes
* Competitors
* Workarounds

Determine:

* Why current solutions fail
* Why users switch
* Why users stay

## Validation Questions

Determine:

* Is this a real problem?
* Is the pain severe enough?
* Is there willingness to pay?
* Is automation justified?
* Is AI justified?
* Is this a feature or a business?

If the problem is weak:

Challenge the premise before documenting it.

# PHASE 15 — PRD QUALITY GATE

Reject the PRD if:

* Ambiguity remains
* MVP is unclear
* Requirements are vague
* Success metrics are missing
* Risks are missing
* Assumptions are hidden
* Edge cases are ignored
* Architecture lacks justification
* Cost considerations are absent
* Security considerations are absent

If any condition fails:

Regenerate before finalizing.
